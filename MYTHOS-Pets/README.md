# MYTHOS Pets 0.1.0
Minecraft 1.21.1 / Forge 52.1.5 / Java 21. Standalone, vanilla clients by design. 21 definitions (18 moved from mounts, 3 added); bat, vex and snow golem locked pending safe controllers. One active pet and independently one active mount. Persistent UUID ownership, exclusive shop/reward acquisition, no wild unlocks, pet riding denied. Commands: /pet list, catalog, shop, buy <id>, summon <id>, dismiss; operator /pet credit <player> <amount>, /pet reward <player> <id> <source>. Separate test wallet, not RPG economy. Owner-initiated combat or hostile attack on owner selects nearby Monster target for 200 ticks; no player targets, allied mobs or marked pets/mounts. Cooldown-based pet-attributed damage, poison, slow, weakness, fire, healing, resistance, speed and luck effects. No actual bleeding, critical strike, armor reduction, damage absorption or scout ability yet. Support effects use vanilla effects and healing; luck is not a custom loot multiplier. AI disabled, invulnerable ephemeral pet, collision-checked follow with repositioning; special vanilla ticks and combat behavior require live testing. No pet progression, GUI, custom animations, shared economy, automated boss rewards or RPG XP attribution yet. No persistent cooldowns across resummon except initial full ability delay. Storage world/mythos-pets.json; atomic synchronous save, fail closed. Mount PR #50 independently removes companion entries; no migration for unreleased development stores. CI and live verification pending.

Players can name any owned pet using `/pet rename <id> <name>` (1–24 characters, spaces allowed), and restore the default with `/pet resetname <id>`. Names persist in the same atomic ownership store, update active nameplates immediately, and never change the pet ID or acquisition restrictions. Duplicate display names are allowed. No formatting codes or control characters.

## 0.3.1 — experimental smooth Mooncat mesh
The existing premium_mooncat uses a procedural smooth-normal polygon renderer, not a cuboid approximation. Four 32x20 surfaces, 29 20x12 surfaces and 12 12x8 surfaces emit 10,672 quads (21,344 nominal triangles including degenerate pole triangles). Unit meshes are cached. Head tracking, independently articulated legs and knees, breathing and a six-segment tail are animated. The embedded 64x64 texture is sampled as a material palette; this is not a fully UV-painted production model. No new dependency, ownership change or change to other pets. Install matching 0.3.1 JARs on client and server. Verify close-up visuals, movement, tail, lighting, and frame rate with multiple visible pets before accepting this experimental renderer.

## 0.3.2 — cinematic Mooncat experiment

## 0.4.0 — reference-inspired asset foundation
Dragon torso, neck, belly, skull and jaw profiles are embedded in assets/mythospets/meshes/ember_dragon.anatomy. The client reads and validates the profiles lazily and builds UV-mapped ring surfaces with distance-based resolution. A deterministic Java build-time generator produces a 2048x2048 material atlas with scale, horn and membrane patterns. This is a custom parametric asset format, not glTF/GLB or a weighted skeletal model. Existing joint animation and fullbright accents remain. Normal maps, separate emissive maps, Blender import, animation blending and professional sculpting are future work. Test appearance and resource loading in-game. No ownership or server gameplay changes.

## 0.4.1 — cinematic geometry experiment
Close-range geometry includes 12,096 raised six-sided scale patches (18 nondegenerate triangles per patch) across torso, neck, skull and jaw, plus 16 double-sided wing membrane patches with 32 subdivisions per edge. The nominal combined scale/membrane budget is 250,496 triangles, excluding the underlying anatomy, limbs, horns and other details. Actual visible geometry varies with occlusion and pose. Scale meshes are cached CPU-side; they are not GPU-instanced. LOD thresholds are 5, 10 and 20 blocks, with raised scales disabled beyond 20. The 4096x4096 atlas includes procedural scale and membrane-vein detail. Existing animation, ownership and pet abilities are retained. This remains a procedural articulated mesh, not a sculpted weighted skeletal asset; normal maps and separate emissive textures are not included. Client FPS and visuals must be tested before deployment.

### Cinematic idle performances (0.4.1)
Server-synchronized idle actions cycle through wing stretch/flap (64 ticks), hind-leg rear (84 ticks), and rear/roar (72 ticks). First action after 100 quiet ticks; subsequent actions after 100–240 quiet ticks. Position changes cancel the action. The renderer eases chest elevation about the hind hips, counter-rotates hind legs and tail, curls forelegs, opens the jaw for roaring, and animates wing shoulder/elbow with phase offsets. Roar plays the vanilla Ender Dragon growl once at tick 26; no new sound asset or combat effect. These are cosmetic articulated poses, not physical flight or weighted skeletal deformation. Movement cancellation, sound timing, multiplayer synchronization, foot placement and wing clipping require runtime validation.

## 0.4.5 — Advanced articulated animation

## 0.4.7 — Bat-inspired wing rig
Replaces the previous outer wing fan with articulated humerus, forearm, wrist and four two-segment digits per wing. Sixteen membrane patches per wing share the digit joints and deform along their local surface normals, with variable tension and travelling flutter. ## 0.4.8 — Smooth idle flight

## 0.6.0 — Pet progression
All catalog pets have independent persistent owner/type XP (existing ownership and names preserved; existing pets start at level 1). Max level 50; cumulative threshold 50*(level-1)*level; 25 XP per qualifying hostile kill credited directly to owner or active pet. Owner kills require current engagement; pet within 32 blocks and owner within 64 blocks of kill. No passive XP or PvP XP; indirect fire deaths may not qualify. XP saved atomically on each award; a storage error locks the existing store. XP retained when a pet is revoked and reacquired by the same account. Stats per level after 1: attack and healing +2.5% of base, agility +0.75%, haste +0.6% (cooldown divided by haste), effect duration +1.2%. Pets remain invulnerable; no misleading health/armor stat. Base abilities remain available at level 1. At 10/25/40 unlock three automatic additional moves; shared 300-tick base cooldown scaled by haste, level 10/25 alternate, level 40 prioritized when owner health <=65%. Additional moves use particles/sounds rather than unique full skeletal animations. All require a valid nearby hostile engagement. Commands: /pet stats [id], /pet moves [id], /pet xp grant <player> <id> <amount> (permission 4). Nameplates and owned lists show level. Progression math checked in CI; runtime persistence, combat attribution and visual effects require testing.

## 0.6.1 — Advanced move tier balance
Replaces the alternating level 10/25 rotation with highest unlocked offensive tier priority. Level 40 emergency move takes priority at owner health <=65%. Dragon Ward replaces Phoenix Ward. Base advanced damage: dragon 4/8 HP at 10/25; mooncat 3/5 HP; others 3/4 HP. Level 25 dragon adds seven seconds burning and Weakness I; other pets add Weakness I, Absorption II and Resistance I. Level 40 heals 6 HP (dragon/others) or 8 HP (mooncat), Resistance II and Absorption II, plus Fire Resistance for dragon or Regeneration II for mooncat. Shared advanced cooldown base 15/13/20 seconds for tiers 10/25/40, scaled by haste. Earlier starting abilities remain available. All damage, healing and supported effect duration still scale with pet level. This supersedes the rotation and advanced cooldown descriptions in 0.6.0. Runtime balance not yet tested.
