# MYTHOS Mounts 0.1.0
Forge 52.1.5 / Minecraft 1.21.1 / Java 21. Vanilla clients by design.
38 mount definitions; 18 companion-oriented entries moved to MYTHOS Pets. Native horse-family/camel steering requires validation. Other ground, flight and water mounts use /mount travel toggling look-directed motion; flying/swimming are experimental, not WASD. Water mounts require nearby water at summon and remain in water; no underwater breathing grant. Small mobs have scaled bodies. Five special mobs (Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden, Enderman, Giant) are catalog-only and blocked from purchase/reward/summon until dedicated safe controllers exist. No custom models. Runtime testing required.
Commands: /mount list, shop, catalog, buy <id>, summon <id>, dismiss, travel. Operators: /mount credit <player> <amount>, /mount reward <player> <id> <source>. Reward source must match catalog; SHOP mounts cannot be rewarded. No wild acquisition, breeding unlock or transfer. All player riding of living entities is denied unless an owned active mount, including mounts from other mods; nonliving vehicles allowed. Tagged mounts are ephemeral and invalid on restart. Summon ownership persistent, one active per player, dismiss on logout/dimension/death. Native mob AI disabled; invulnerable summons. No combat abilities. 
Economy is a separate mount-gold balance for testing; not MYTHOS RPG gold. Shop is command-based and global, not merchant NPC GUI. No real-money currency. Rewards are operator-triggered integration entry points, not automatic quest/boss listeners. Catalog and prices are initial code-defined defaults. Storage atomic replacement on server thread, fail-closed on error; no cross-module transactions. Back up world/mythos-mounts.json. CI and in-game testing pending.
